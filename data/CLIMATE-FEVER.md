This index is adapted from CLIMATE-FEVER by Thomas Diggelmann, Jordan Boyd-Graber, Jannis Bulian, Massimiliano Ciaramita and Markus Leippold (2020). Each row contains the original claim, its aggregate annotation and its original line number. The linked original record contains the evidence sentences and individual votes. These are historical research annotations.
